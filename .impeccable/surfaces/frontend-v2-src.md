## Direction contract

Scope: whole app (front door = Persuade; traveller and agent workspaces = Operate). Code-led, no image generation. Chosen by the user: Modular Identity System (Studio Dumbar).

THESIS: The plan is a stack of colour bars on one module grid, and a change is a bar that snaps in, strikes out or slides, always tied to its reason. It refuses the grey SaaS dashboard of same-size cards and explainability as a wall of paragraphs.

OWN-WORLD: Cool grey ground (#E7EAEE) with ink (#101418) type. Four unmixed bar colours that always mean something: cyan = action and AI, green = added or approved, magenta = removed or needs attention, yellow = shifted or pending. Archivo variable, 800 for display with key words set on colour bars, 14px businesslike UI text, tabular numerals. One 8px module and a 12-column grid; flat fills, multiply overlaps, 1px rules, no shadows. Loose confetti marks only on the front door. Photography is replaced by the real stop coordinates drawn as a flat route plate.

STORY: In one screen a visitor understands that feedback re-plans a group trip and that every change is explained. Travellers see what moved and why; agents compare options as bars and approve. They explore the demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 12-column grid. Left five columns: headline "Plans that explain their own changes." with "explain" on a cyan bar, one sentence, ink "Explore the demo" button and a quiet "Sign in". Right seven columns: a route plate of real Delhi stops with a day's before and after bars beneath it. A three-step strip sits at the fold. Confetti marks cluster at the plate's corner.

FORM: Studio Dumbar modular identity system, a user-chosen challenger (catalog studio-dumbar-identity), not on my own list. seed:4b605794dbf98cb6

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
